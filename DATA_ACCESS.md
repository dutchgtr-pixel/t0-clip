# Proprietary research data and access requests

The project's working datasets are proprietary research assets. They are not
included in this public repository and are not available as public downloads.
The software license does not grant access to, or redistribution rights over,
the private datasets.

Private assets include Parquet datasets, row-level observations, original record
identifiers, source links, seller information, training exports, fitted private
vocabularies and trained private checkpoints. These must not be committed to this
repository, attached to public issues, or placed in public releases.

Researchers may contact the repository maintainer to request access. A request
should describe the research purpose, affiliation where applicable, required
fields and time periods, intended analysis, and proposed data-handling environment.
Access requires the maintainer's explicit approval and separately agreed access
or licensing terms. A request does not automatically grant access. Any approved
delivery must use a separate controlled channel, not this public repository.

The public research package contains source code, documentation, diagrams,
aggregate results, artificial examples, and two expressly approved anonymous
photographs with limited illustrative judgments. Those photographs do not expose
their original record identifiers, source URLs or seller details, and do not make
the underlying image collection or tabular datasets public. They are illustrative
media, not an openly released training dataset.

Published counts and metrics summarize retained evidence. They cannot be used to
reconstruct or download the private observation tables. Researchers seeking to
reproduce the historical real-data experiments need an approved data agreement
and the corresponding versioned artifacts; the public synthetic examples alone
are not a substitute for that access.
